## Description (4000 char max — first ~170 are the "above the fold" hook)

```
Read the tell. Land the combo. Knock down what's haunting you.

Ghost Puncher is a first-person punch-em-up against the supernatural — rebuilt from the ground up for 2026 after eight years off the App Store.

Every fight is a reading puzzle. Each opponent telegraphs their attacks: an eye-flash means a quick jab, an arm pulled back means a heavy hook, a body crouch means a slam you'd better dodge. Read the tell, time your block, and punish the recovery. Or panic and eat the punch — your call.

THE FIGHT FEEL
• Cancel-window combos — chain punches at the right cadence for a damage multiplier
• Stamina meter that rewards rhythm over mashing
• Core Haptics — feel every connection, including the chain bonus
• 120Hz on ProMotion iPhones

THE BOSSES
• The Ghost — vanishes mid-fight and strikes from the dark. Tells shorten as she's hurt.
• The Witch — feints constantly. Yellow flash = fake. Punish her recovery.
• The Devil — slow, heavy slams. Phase two kicks in at half-health.
• The Hidden Boss — three phases. By phase three, the tells are half as long as they were in phase one.

DESIGNED FOR YOUR PHONE
• Native iOS — no tracking, no accounts, no internet required
• Single-player, offline, no microtransactions
• iPhone-only, landscape, iOS 17+

WHAT'S NEW IN 2026
A complete rebuild over the 2016 original. Swift 5, iOS 17+, ProMotion 120Hz, Core Haptics, a real telegraph-based AI movekit per opponent, cancel windows, and a phase-aware final boss.
```

_(Above is ~1500 chars. Apple's hard ceiling is 4000.)_
