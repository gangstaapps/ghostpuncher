# Ghost Puncher — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: June 18, 2026_

## Short version

Ghost Puncher collects **no personal data**. No account is required. No
analytics, no tracking, no advertising SDKs. The app does not connect to
any server.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for, transmit, or store any personally
identifying information. There are no accounts, no logins, no telemetry,
and no third-party SDKs included in the build.

## What the app saves locally

The app uses Apple's standard `UserDefaults` to remember small pieces of
in-game progress on your device — for example, the current level you've
reached. This data:

- Stays on your device
- Is never transmitted anywhere
- Is removed when you delete the app

## Third parties

We do not use:

- Analytics services (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
- Advertising networks
- Crash reporting services
- User identifier collection (no IDFA usage)
- Social or external login services

## Children's privacy

The app is rated 9+ and does not collect any data — from children or
adults. It is safe to use without any data-handling concerns.

## Changes

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will appear at this
URL with a new "Last updated" date. We will not change the data-handling
behavior of the app without updating this page first.
